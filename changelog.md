## SalemStudy 0.3.5

### Improved
- **No more cards on the professor's jokes.** Flashcards, quizzes and notes now leave out what is not course material: a meme or cartoon on the first or last slide, an anecdote, a famous person or event brought in only to lighten the lecture, a "fun fact", or course admin like office hours. It still counts when the course actually teaches it - a historical figure in a history course, or the scientist behind a law you are examined on.
- **Slides with nothing to learn stay empty.** Going page by page, a title slide, a joke or a recap that got no cards is no longer sent back to be written for, so nothing gets squeezed out of it.
