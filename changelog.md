## SalemStudy 0.3.2

### New
- **Choose which diagrams become quiz questions.** With "Label diagrams" on, Make a quiz finds every labelled diagram in the sources you tick and shows them as pictures, grouped by source and page. Untick the ones you don't want; every one you keep becomes a question.
- **Sources open where you are.** The "From…" line under a quiz answer or on a flashcard is now a link: it opens that page of the source in a sheet beside the question, even mid-quiz.
- **Flashcards show how each card went.** Playing a deck now has the same strip as quizzes, one segment per card, green for got it and red for missed it. Click one to jump to that card.

### Improved
- **Going back to a card shows what you marked.** A card you already marked comes back answer side up, with your choice highlighted. Pick again to change it.
- **See your progress in the card list.** While a deck is part way through, its list of cards shows how each one went in that run.
- **Fill-the-gap answers forgive spelling.** A misspelt or differently worded right answer is now checked by the AI and counted as right, with a note on how it is spelt.
- **Questions list shows their pictures.** Viewing all questions in a quiz now shows each question's figure or diagram.
- **The note toolbar wraps.** In a narrow window the Notes toolbar moves onto a second row instead of cutting tools off.
- **"Checked in Python" only when it was.** Questions that were not checked no longer say "unverified".

### Fixed
- **Chat windows close when you click outside them.** Clicking the dimmed area around a chat opened from a quiz or deck now closes it, instead of dragging the app window.
- **Quizzes and decks fill the window with the sidebar folded.** On a Mac, starting one with the sidebar folded left a strip of sidebar behind, and the window buttons stayed small.
- **Windows corners line up.** The page's rounded corners now sit evenly inside the window's own.
- **One outline on the memory search.** Searching your memories in Settings no longer draws two rings around the box.
