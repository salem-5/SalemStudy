## SalemStudy 0.3.0

### New
- **A whole new look.** Calmer and quieter from top to bottom: one set of colours, spacing and shapes across every page, in light, dark or whatever your system uses. Colour is kept for what means something - right and wrong, deadlines, and each subject's own colour. Your accent colour is set back to the first one once, since the old ones were picked for colours that no longer exist; pick a new one in Settings.
- **Home starts with your subjects,** then the three quizzes and decks you were last in the middle of, so you can carry straight on.
- **Paste straight into Sources.** Press ⌘V (Ctrl+V on Windows) on the Sources page to add what you copied: images and files are added as they are, and text becomes a file of its own with a proper title. A YouTube link is added as a video.
- **On Windows the window has no title bar,** like on a Mac. Move the pointer to the top of the window and the page slides down to show the minimise, maximise and close buttons.
- **On a Mac the window buttons sit in the sidebar,** and close up neatly when you fold it.
- **Diagram labelling is part of setup.** The text recognition it needs is installed along with everything else, so turning on "Label diagrams" no longer asks for a separate download. If it is missing, Salem's setup window shows to finish it.

### Improved
- **Making flashcards, quizzes and notes is simpler.** The dialog shows what matters - what to use, how much, how hard - with the rest under "More options". "From a topic" now says plainly that it does not use your sources.
- **Shorter titles.** Decks, quizzes and notes are named in a few words by what they cover, without your file names or lecture numbers in front.
- **A reworked schedule.** Events show in their course's colour, the chosen day lists its events on clearer cards, and "Coming up" fits each one on a single line.
- **Quizzes and decks pick up where you should.** Reopening one takes you to the first question or card you have not answered yet, not the last one you clicked past.
- **Smoother movement.** Going into a quiz or deck and back out is one quick, smooth move, and switching tabs in a notebook slides the way you are going.
- **Your activity** shows the last eight weeks in bigger squares, and the notebook chat's controls sit on their own bar above the conversation.
- **Tidier everywhere:** every page keeps the same distance from the sidebar and the edge of the window, menus open where you clicked with room between icon and text, and Settings buttons look like buttons.

### Fixed
- Clicking a day in "Your activity" always said nothing was recorded.
- The "…" menus on flashcard and quiz cards could open in the wrong place.
- Keyboard shortcuts show Ctrl instead of ⌘ on Windows and Linux.
- Switched-on toggles in dark mode looked like a plain white pill.
- A dark box showed behind the message box in chats opened over a quiz or card.
