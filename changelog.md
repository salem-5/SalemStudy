## SalemStudy 0.2.2

### New
- **Diagram labels are marked on meaning, not spelling.** When you label a diagram, any box that misses the expected word is now read by the AI, which accepts it when it names the same thing: a synonym, the name another textbook uses, an abbreviation or its expansion, a singular where the label is plural, or a plain typo. Boxes it accepts turn green and the feedback says which ones and why. It can only turn a wrong box right, never take away one you already had, and a box you left empty is never sent. Written answers were already marked this way; diagrams now are too.
- **Start over from a saved quiz or deck.** A quiz or deck you left part-way through now offers **Start over** beside **Carry on**, so you can take it again from the first question without working through what you already answered. It asks first, and your finished attempts and scores are left alone.

### Improved
- **Decks say how far you got.** A deck you left part-way through now shows **Carry on (N seen)** instead of just **Play deck**, so it is clear it will pick up where you stopped.
- **A finished quiz no longer pretends to be a fresh one.** A quiz you have finished but not closed says **Review answers**, which is what the button actually did.
