## SalemStudy 0.3.1

### Improved
- **Folding a subject in the sidebar keeps the notebook you are in.** Its other notebooks tuck away and the open one stays under the subject until you leave it. Opening a notebook in a folded subject shows just that notebook, instead of unfolding the whole subject.

### Fixed
- **Diagrams with a see-through background now show on white.** Label-the-diagram questions made from pictures with transparency - common in PDFs and slides - showed them on solid black. They now sit on white, as they do in your lecture. Diagrams in quizzes you have already made keep their old background; make the quiz again to fix them.
- **On Windows, the window controls get out of the way sooner.** Once the pointer leaves the strip at the top of the window, the minimise, maximise and close buttons tuck away almost at once instead of lingering.
