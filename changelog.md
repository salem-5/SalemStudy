## SalemStudy 0.2.3

### New
- **A label you get wrong now tells you why.** When a diagram label is marked wrong, it says in a sentence what the thing you named actually is and how it differs from the right label, instead of only showing you the word you missed. A label accepted as the same answer under another name says what let it through - synonym, abbreviation, spelling slip - so you know it was not a fluke. It comes from the same check that marks the labels, so it costs no extra wait.

### Improved
- **The answer card for a diagram question is far easier to read.** It used to show the same list of labels up to four times over: your answers with ticks, the right answers, the score, and an "explanation" that was only the answers listed again. Now there is one line per label - what you typed, the right label where they differ, and the explanation underneath - and nothing repeated. Labels you got right on the first try are left off entirely, so only what you need to look at is there.
